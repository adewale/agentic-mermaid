## Selection and rationale

**Use TF+position only as an opt-in for exact repeated workloads.** In the separate
controlled supplement its SVG/all-output scores are **0.718879/0.712570**, versus
all twelve **0.764038/0.795986**, each with its own empty-flag main references.
Repeat is lower in all three pairs on sixteen of twenty-seven cases, with none
consistently higher in that epoch; all twelve has nine consistently lower and a
consistent Pie loss. This is the strongest observed conditional combination,
not a direct marginal-effect estimate, default promotion or 4,096-subset optimum.

The tradeoff is explicit: repeat's twenty-SVG extra-call allocation sum ratio is
0.684995 with **+4,355,968 bytes** median paired final whole-worker heap. All twelve
has a slightly lower allocation ratio, 0.677445, but **+4,660,536 bytes** heap and
known reverse-order/terminal/edit losses. All profiles ship the same candidate
code. No profile wins latency, residency, delivery and every family simultaneously.

Keep the cross-checks visible. The earlier main matrix has repeat losses in
Sequence/Journey SVG and Flow terminal; the supplemental epoch does not erase
those. A/A varies substantially. Browser TF versus ordinary main has adverse
pairs in two rounds, median **1.130545**. Packed release has a favorable corpus
median **0.872598**, but an adverse third round and a 2.228-second tail. Normal-cache
off scores **1.168506 SVG / 1.183321 all** retain default-path risk. Real request
hit rates, family mix and transient memory determine whether the opt-in helps.
