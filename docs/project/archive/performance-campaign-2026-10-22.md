# Performance campaign review

PR #467 retains measured editing/startup and targeted layout improvements, reduces whole-corpus V8 allocation modestly, and fixes six baseline MCP collection failures. It does **not** make Agentic Mermaid faster than Beautiful or close the large gap to Pele. Whole-render timing is mixed, some retained-heap observations worsen, and the production bundle grows slightly. Nine experimental implementations were rejected. Broader workspace, proof-lifetime, routing and distribution changes remain unimplemented and are accounted for below.

The complete baseline preceded production changes. Both selected artifacts completed full 18-phase replays, followed by exact-fixture follow-ups and qualification of the rebase onto main `87c9c317`. The review covers architecture, owning algorithms and history across the repository; it does not claim a line-by-line inspection of every source file.

## How the lineage accumulated cost

Beautiful began on 28 January with Dagre, specialized Sequence placement and direct SVG strings in [3185b9b6](https://github.com/adewale/agentic-mermaid/commit/3185b9b6911e3b0a9f0d51fcebda5e0f69785ff0). [02daec0e](https://github.com/adewale/agentic-mermaid/commit/02daec0ee8fba7dc26f9795386e8835c8d948795) replaced Dagre with bundled ELK on 23 February, adding compound layout and orthogonal routing through a general solver and a substantial static dependency. Its constructor was lazy; its JavaScript import was static. No controlled measurement in that commit establishes a numerical Dagre-to-ELK regression.

[026688ae](https://github.com/adewale/agentic-mermaid/commit/026688ae042472ab395fbc42c8c3b2b832d63b8a) added alignment, post-compaction and bundling. Agentic inherited the pattern of solving geometry and then revising it for renderer constraints. June–July route, port, label and feedback guarantees expanded that work. [566fc5d1](https://github.com/adewale/agentic-mermaid/commit/566fc5d17ca89b91dd23d1077d0fd2cc83b6faf9) made 16 post-ELK steps explicit, including four post-freeze repairs and a stale-endpoint repair net. The manifest clarified ownership; it did not remove rerouting after node movement.

The July Scene/style architecture added common intermediate construction, admission, serialization and output processing. Byte-identity gates protected output but could not detect extra copies, scans or garbage collection. Comparable release, peer and allocation feedback became systematic in October, later than semantic and visual quality feedback. Earlier benchmarks mainly established advantages over browser-process startup and terminal tools; they did not continuously measure equivalent browserless SVG work.

October work already narrowed imports, deferred backends, reduced admission copying, added bounded caches and retained prepared text. [#458](https://github.com/adewale/agentic-mermaid/pull/458) already removed full Scene construction from ordinary native crisp output. Those are baseline improvements, not this campaign's work. The history establishes accumulating execution obligations and late cost attribution, not what individual authors privately knew.

## Why the current pipeline remains expensive

At the campaign baseline, the ELK-compatible graph path converts Map/string authored graphs into ELK-shaped measured objects, layout output and positioned objects, then traverses a [25-entry post-layout manifest](https://github.com/adewale/agentic-mermaid/blob/b09ef80229df42a620cf899054c721f0d7ecfe36/src/layout-engine.ts#L1293). Eligible indexed forests can bypass the external solver. The manifest includes conditional and no-op passes. Several move geometry after routes exist; later stages reanchor, reroute and certify it. [Pass contracts](https://github.com/adewale/agentic-mermaid/blob/b09ef80229df42a620cf899054c721f0d7ecfe36/src/layout/pass.ts#L115) measure declared metrics and snapshot frozen boxes and edge points. These checks protect real constraints, but a no-op or compensating repair can still incur traversal and allocation.

Native crisp output bypasses a full Scene tree but still constructs drawing/paint evidence and applies SVG transformations and security. Sequence bypasses ELK altogether, making it a control for cost outside ELK, including its own parsing, measurement and placement. Source, editable bodies, positioned geometry and SVG also have distinct copying and retention lifetimes. Broader Agentic capabilities account for real work; they cannot explain every gap on shared SVG inputs.

There are specific scaling liabilities. Source ordering and [route classification](https://github.com/adewale/agentic-mermaid/blob/b09ef80229df42a620cf899054c721f0d7ecfe36/src/route-contracts.ts#L217) can perform reachability searches per edge, admitting O(E(V+E)) worst-case work. Forward classifications often resolve cheaply; the rejected classification-cache experiment demonstrates why that bound alone does not justify an index on every input. Route/node and label-pair checks add potentially quadratic scans. These are source-derived bounds, not measured profile shares. Updating one overlap index does not make all routing or verification linear. The campaign removes some concrete copying, discarded scoring and repeated inspection; it does not remove this entire architecture.

## What the peers demonstrate

The [dated community inventory](https://github.com/adewale/agentic-mermaid/blob/b09ef80229df42a620cf899054c721f0d7ecfe36/docs/mermaid-community.json) contains **83 nodes/64 edges**. It distinguishes shared-root forks, acknowledged ports, bindings and independent implementations; it is not an 83-algorithm leaderboard. The separate fork-graph benchmark fixture has **81 nodes/63 edges**, including Sketch, and must not silently replace that inventory. Beautiful/Agentic share ancestry; Ondine explicitly ports Pele.

Pele shows why implementation language alone is an inadequate explanation: it is TypeScript too. Its [layered kernel](https://github.com/obsidianmd/pele/blob/4d1be9d5f3708596c27fcb3878715d0c699eae91/src/layout/layered.ts#L417) uses dense IDs, CSR adjacency, contiguous rank order and typed arrays as the algorithms' working representation. Iterative DFS orients cycles; longest paths plus bounded relaxation assign ranks; reusable inversion structures and bounded barycenter sweeps reduce crossings; Brandes–Köpf assigns coordinates. Caps limit dummy expansion, expensive weighting and repeated ordering.

The [Flowchart adapter](https://github.com/obsidianmd/pele/blob/4d1be9d5f3708596c27fcb3878715d0c699eae91/src/diagrams/flowchart/render.ts#L93) supplies measured dimensions, label footprints and minimum spans before compound layout and SVG emission; [core render](https://github.com/obsidianmd/pele/blob/4d1be9d5f3708596c27fcb3878715d0c699eae91/src/core.ts#L147) bounds output. Fitting and compound retries remain. The distinction is fewer representation handoffs and bounded effort, not a literally single-pass renderer. An extra typed-array conversion around unchanged owning algorithms would not reproduce that benefit.

Source inspection here uses Pele **1.8.0**, commit `4d1be9d5`; the campaign measures published **Pele 1.6.0**, **Beautiful 1.1.3** and **Ondine 0.0.1**. The published Pele bundle confirms numeric workspaces and bounded sweeps, without proving byte parity with newer source. Ondine's [Node compiler](https://github.com/wevm/ondine/blob/f662aa795b91bf2dfc7162f48035be3884201193/src/compiler/index.ts#L22) lazily instantiates a speed-optimized all-family WASM module. Its [bridge](https://github.com/wevm/ondine/blob/f662aa795b91bf2dfc7162f48035be3884201193/src/internal/Wasm.ts#L85) resets state, allocates source/options together and encodes directly into linear memory. This reduces JavaScript object traffic, but V8 allocation omits WASM memory. Mount-only delivery displays precompiled SVG; compiler download and initialization are separate costs.

## Baseline and measurement boundaries

The full baseline finished on 22 October at 20:40:10 UTC, before production implementation: source `b09ef80229df42a620cf899054c721f0d7ecfe36`, detached maintained harness `d7c929d8b45e635299cfc775e50ebc5fda8bf27d`, frozen package SHA-256 `77773fea91b8dbe80a5345f36e6f079f4816cad8ba535fdf693cf2cf8f3cc75b`. Harness production changes were not merged to create the baseline.

Both cache regimes completed the 563-input release suite, maintained and consumed-output peer workloads, 1,095 page occurrences in Node and browser, memory runs, payload graphs and targeted extras. Completion is not universal acceptance: the release-on Sequence latency gate failed, six public MCP Map cases failed, and unsupported/lost-label rows remain recorded. Sketch initially failed with the available Go compiler; a pinned Go 1.25.3 supplement repaired the environment while preserving the failed run. The later Sequence A/A pass does not erase the original failure.

On the full matched browser accepted population, the median ratios of same-round corpus-total time were 31.76× off and 37.78× on for AM/Pele; AM/Mermaid ratios were 0.159× and 0.182×. These belong to that protocol and denominator, not an old quick-run ranking. Acceptance and surviving labels do not prove equivalent edges, ports, containment or aesthetics. Plain output, consumed output and full receipts remain separate. Likewise, the 5,493-byte gzip initial loader differs from Sequence's 211,160-byte static closure and Flowchart's 711,574-byte closure including conditional ELK. Module compression is not network latency; sampled JS allocation is not whole-engine memory.
