## Frankenmermaid: transferable source ideas

Fresh public outcomes remain the deciding evidence: complete consumed SVG and complete serialized receipts, fixed supported cohorts, both cache policies, calibrated controls, and explicit adverse/unavailable cells. Source abstractions suggest experiments; they establish neither measured gains nor a Frankenmermaid ranking. This review pins Frankenmermaid to [`f1c2fe260dfc43ffc2e40a423cb4c3b6206bfadf`](https://github.com/Dicklesworthstone/frankenmermaid/commit/f1c2fe260dfc43ffc2e40a423cb4c3b6206bfadf) and compares Agentic **bc8f1bd0**. 1. **Join admission and owned emission — #433/shared renderer.** Frankenmermaid’s [document builder](https://github.com/Dicklesworthstone/frankenmermaid/blob/f1c2fe260dfc43ffc2e40a423cb4c3b6206bfadf/crates/fm-render-svg/src/document.rs#L265) writes into one final buffer; its [renderer](https://github.com/Dicklesworthstone/frankenmermaid/blob/f1c2fe260dfc43ffc2e40a423cb4c3b6206bfadf/crates/fm-render-svg/src/lib.rs#L5510) preserves child order and retains fallbacks. Agentic already has an append-only per-mark writer, shallow native drawing and lazy receipts. The remaining removal is intermediate marks/fragments, recovery and repeated traversal across admission, emission and occurrence recording. Start with one Sequence slice sharing admitted values through final transformation. Preserve eager error/byte-budget order, raw-text coverage, affine arithmetic and final transformed-byte security; do not copy f32 formatting. Measure fresh consumed latency/allocation and output parity.

2. **Reuse dense graph workspaces — #449 solver, shared adapter boundary.** [`BarycenterScratch`](https://github.com/Dicklesworthstone/frankenmermaid/blob/f1c2fe260dfc43ffc2e40a423cb4c3b6206bfadf/crates/fm-layout/src/lib.rs#L17561) builds flat incidence once, preserves edge order/multiplicity and reuses storage. Agentic already has indexed paths and heap-based source ordering. The opportunity is carrying one render-owned ancestry/incidence workspace through conversion, classification and repair, removing rebuilt maps and repeated scans. Preserve authored IDs, stable ties, ports, self/parallel edges and compounds. Coordinate with #449 rather than introduce a competing solver. Measure graph scaling/allocation and complete Flowchart latency; this is not Sequence’s non-ELK opportunity.

3. **Share conservative obstacle indexes — #449 routing; #433 for proof lifetime.** Frankenmermaid’s [`ObstacleSpatialIndex`](https://github.com/Dicklesworthstone/frankenmermaid/blob/f1c2fe260dfc43ffc2e40a423cb4c3b6206bfadf/crates/fm-layout/src/lib.rs#L19274) combines flat cells, generation-stamped deduplication, reusable candidates and linear fallback before exact filtering. Agentic already has broad phases. Remaining work is an index owned by frozen geometry or explicit mutation epochs, removing repeated construction across checks. Preserve conservative candidate coverage, exact predicates and deterministic diagnostic selection; approximate cursor proximity is unsuitable. Measure dense-graph tails/allocation including construction and small-case costs.

4. **Invalidate by actual dependencies — #449 incremental layout/#433 evidence lifetime.** The active incremental path’s [`metrics_topology_equal`](https://github.com/Dicklesworthstone/frankenmermaid/blob/f1c2fe260dfc43ffc2e40a423cb4c3b6206bfadf/crates/fm-layout/src/lib.rs#L1676) checks exact dependencies for graph metrics. Remove additional dirty-phase recomputation and snapshots only where dependencies justify it; topology equality cannot authorize unchanged labels, geometry, paint or transformed bytes. 5. **Record computed work — #433 measurement.** [`ReuseProof`](https://github.com/Dicklesworthstone/frankenmermaid/blob/f1c2fe260dfc43ffc2e40a423cb4c3b6206bfadf/crates/fm-core/src/reuse_proof.rs#L33) distinguishes requested/computed work; the type itself measures nothing. Bind benchmark claims to executed APIs and full consumption, not comments or returned strings. This improves attribution, not assumed production speed; retain maintained controls and adverse observations.
