## First-stage trace and implementation constraints

`src/layout/native-nesting.ts:42–60` deliberately declines global leaf-aware nesting when the sibling quotient is acyclic. Here that quotient is A→B→C. `src/layout/agentic-compound-core.ts:85–105` then places each group's leaves with no hosted internal edges, so the child ordering cannot see the cross-group permutations. The parent sees only lifted group edges. Frozen geometry is joined at `compound-boundary-routes.ts:719` onward.

This establishes loss of ordering information, but the precise cause of the support refusal still needs control inputs: identity permutations and a two-group subset may also hit the visibility strategy. In particular, the small path uses a cumulative Cartesian-grid eligibility budget (`compound-boundary-routes.ts:857–863`), whereas more than16 lifted edges take the direct-only family (`:679`). Do not mistake an information-loss trace for proof that changing ordering alone cures the exception.

The previous unconditional acyclic-DAG global nesting trial was withdrawn after9/25 compound quality regressions (work-log,10November). Do not restore it indiscriminately. Any extension must have a structural reason, retain actual mixed directions/model order/frame endpoints/cycles compatibility, and preserve existing work accounting and caps. Prefer making an existing placement or route strategy own the decision once over adding another late repair.

The owning regression should require all four authored directions to deliver with Native selected and no fallback, unchanged source/input meaning and group memberships, actual leaf endpoint attachment and direction, no node/header/label/marker obstruction, and complete final paint/receipt certification where the admitted domain supports it. Crossings/bends/wire/area are recorded against this baseline and the existing compound corpus; ELK coordinates are not expected values. Do not enforce zero crossings merely because the simpler peer test does: our header and marker contracts differ. Keep operation limits, deterministic repeat behaviour, UTF16 identity and caller mutability/getter semantics unchanged.

## Causal controls and routing findings

Sol's isolated source-loader controls return7/12 at baseline: the four named directions, four identity-permutation directions and four two-group controls. Identity three-group TB/BT also fail, so permutation alone does not explain support refusal. Forcing existing global nesting returns12/12 but repeats known quality tradeoffs (namedTB area+32%; identityLR/RL wire roughly+89%). Unconditional global admission remains withdrawn. This does not rule out every bounded Native selection strategy; it rules out claiming global ordering is a free universal fix.

The narrow routing trace found LR/RL failing near edge7 after roughly31.8k expanded storage, within the unchanged32,768 capacity. At `compound-boundary-routes.ts:785–789`, primary obstacles include pending lifted edges' temporary quotient paths. The existing fallback at885–887 already excludes those unfinalized peers. Applying that ownership exclusion alone fails0/12 because it exposes a hard `no clear anchor` exception: a completed physical route can obstruct the next temporary quotient anchor. Recovering only this geometric primary miss, while preserving capacity/type failures and final endpoint proof, together with the exclusion returns9/12. Anchor recovery alone remains7/12. The remaining failures are the three vertical cases. All scratch variants are retained by Sol in `compound-implementation`; none of these counts is a performance claim.
