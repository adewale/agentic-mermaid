## Recommendation

Do not turn the seven suggestions into seven implementation projects. Preserve the overlapping fixes during integration, then prefer one small candidate-provenance correction in the existing Native producer/certification boundary. A universal solver abstraction is not the most compelling next change: new main already has shared selection policy, conformance tests at several boundaries, and deliberate family-owned geometry. Producer clearance remains a worthwhile subsequent geometry investigation, but our diamond intervention was not a demonstrated quality improvement.

## What “no neutral solver contract” can accurately mean

[backend.ts:57–90](https://github.com/adewale/agentic-mermaid/blob/c366de0d137e33e123acd84fc99164417c0f3917/src/layout/backend.ts#L57) is already a shared selection/fallback/provenance contract. It takes two `() => T` callbacks, preserves typed-decline behavior and optionally certified candidate rejection. [measured-backend.test.ts:5–54](https://github.com/adewale/agentic-mermaid/blob/c366de0d137e33e123acd84fc99164417c0f3917/src/__tests__/measured-backend.test.ts#L5) already tests that contract, including unexpected/deferred-error propagation and immutable fallback evidence.

It does not define common solver input or output: [Native MeasuredGraph/AgenticLayout](https://github.com/adewale/agentic-mermaid/blob/c366de0d137e33e123acd84fc99164417c0f3917/src/layout/agentic-layered-core.ts#L14) differ from [elkLayoutSync(ElkNode): ElkNode](https://github.com/adewale/agentic-mermaid/blob/c366de0d137e33e123acd84fc99164417c0f3917/src/elk-instance.ts#L93). ELK still uses its private worker dispatcher/global initialization adapter. `backend-capability-conformance.test.ts` concerns Default/Rough/Hybrid **paint backends**, not interchangeable layout solvers. Thus the owner's earlier statement is accurate with this qualification; “we have no shared contract or conformance tests” would be false.

## Why candidate provenance is the smallest defensible next task

The earlier d8c8 ladder witness had three preparation/solver invocations, but selected-candidate recertification replaced its internal comparison evidence with one generic candidate. This is an observability mismatch against placement-production documentation, **not a visible layout correctness failure**. Public backend attempts correctly count selected/fallback backend invocations; final paint attempts are a third independent counter. A targeted change should retain selection history separately from fresh final certification and leave both other meanings unchanged. Acceptance should cover one existing multi-candidate witness, unchanged emitted geometry/meaning, and no invented backend attempts.

The diamond is less ready for production: the traced excess came from repeated active-channel minimum-gap floors, not positive-height dummy bands or failure to halve rank separation. Our compensated intervention preserved physical node separation and passed final SVG/contact/meaning checks, but moved the sink and increased bends from two to four. It remains an allocation/selection investigation, not an approved compaction fix. These conclusions and all performance claims remain tied to their recorded revision; no new Pele speed comparison or rebased-result qualification was performed here.
